Three stages, every interaction

The data flow your AI provider never sees

01

Sent

Your team types a prompt containing client names, case numbers, financial figures — sensitive details included.

02

Sanitized

AKILA replaces every identified sensitive field with a non-reversible placeholder — on your machine, before anything leaves.

03

Restored

The AI responds to the placeholders. AKILA restores your original values for display. Nothing sensitive transited.
